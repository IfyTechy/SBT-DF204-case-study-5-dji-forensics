# DJI Mavic Air — Visual Examination Summary

## 1. Scope

This document summarises selected-frame visual examinations of five
videos extracted from the EWF microSD image and one Android cached video.

The observations are limited to sampled frames and do not represent
a complete frame-by-frame examination.

## 2. EWF microSD video observations

### DJI_0001.MP4
Sampled frames show indoor environments resembling a classroom,
computer laboratory, office or technical workspace. Visible objects
include desks, monitors, cabinets and technical-looking equipment.

No individuals were confidently identified. The exact location,
equipment purpose and activities remain undetermined.

### DJI_0002.MP4
Two people are visible in the sampled footage, one in the foreground
and another near a doorway. Computer workstations, monitors and
storage cabinets are also visible.

Their identities, roles and activities cannot be established.

### DJI_0003.MP4
Sampled aerial views show dry open terrain, sparse vegetation,
a possible dry creek bed or wash, distant hills and a road or
cleared path.

### DJI_0004.MP4
Sampled aerial views show a dry open field, distant mountains,
a dirt track and a possible wash or gully. A human-shaped shadow
appears in one frame, but it does not establish the identity
or role of any person.

### DJI_0005.MP4
Sampled aerial views show dry grassland, scrub vegetation,
dirt tracks and distant hills. Some dark objects may be livestock,
but this interpretation remains unconfirmed.

## 3. Android cached video

Evidence file: `2018_06_19_14_50_39.mp4`

Eight frames were sampled from the approximately four-minute video.

Visible features include open green grassland, a white peaked tent,
people, vehicles, dirt tracks and distant hills.

The identities of people, exact location and nature of the event
were not established.

## 4. Cross-source visual comparison

The Android cached video and EWF videos DJI_0003.MP4,
DJI_0004.MP4 and DJI_0005.MP4 share broad landscape characteristics.

However, the sampled frames do not establish that the Android
video is a copy, segment or continuation of any particular
microSD recording.

Visual similarity alone is insufficient to establish a common
location or flight mission.

## 5. Timestamp observations

The Android cached video's examined QuickTime timestamp fields
contained zero dates and did not provide a usable embedded
recording timestamp.

Its filename contains `14:50:39`, while the TXT flight record
and FLY057 GPS telemetry begin at approximately `18:50:34 UTC`.

The approximately four-hour difference may involve a local-time
versus UTC naming convention, but this explanation remains
unverified.

The filesystem modification time is not treated as the original
recording time.

## 6. Limitations

- Only selected frames were examined.
- No complete frame-by-frame video examination was performed.
- No identities or exact geographical locations were established.
- Possible livestock, structures and other distant objects
  remain unconfirmed.
- Visual similarities do not independently prove that recordings
  belong to the same flight or mission.
- Timestamp interpretations remain subject to further verification.

## 7. Conclusion

The sampled footage contains indoor technical-looking environments
and aerial views of open landscapes. The Android cached video
contains visible people, vehicles and a tent.

These observations provide useful investigative context, but
they do not independently establish the identities of individuals,
the exact recording locations or the relationship between the
Android and microSD recordings.
